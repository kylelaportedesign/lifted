Lifted.
Work with anyone, anywhere, compliantly. The all-in-one mobile workspace for distributed teams.
Brand
Editorial, not corporate.
01
Editorial, magazine-like
Oversized display type, unusual layouts, bento composition. Never a form buried in a card.
02
Yellow is a spotlight
One hottest action per surface. Not a decoration. Reserve #F5F500 for the moment that matters.
03
Human, monogrammed
Contractors are people with initials, cities, currencies. Never rows of stock avatars.
04
Choreographed motion
Payments turn yellow one-by-one as they clear — motion narrates state, doesn't decorate it.
05
Bone base
A warm #F2F0E8 base — never sterile #FFFFFF. Every surface breathes with earth tones.
06
Compliant, quietly
Local rails, MSAs, tax forms — technical rigor with a calm voice. No fear, no jargon.
