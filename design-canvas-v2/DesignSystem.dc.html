Design system · v0.1 · Apr 2026
Foundry.
A shared project workspace where the contractor and the homeowner meet around the render.
Two people, one project, one source of truth. Two density modes on one token set — a dense utility interface for the person who opens it 30 times a day, an editorial surface for the person who opens it six times total.
F
Brand
Six things we won't move on.
01
The render is the shared object
Both people point at the same image from opposite sides. Everything else is scaffolding around that.
02
Two densities, one token set
Contractor UI is dense, utilitarian, thumb-reachable, sunlight-legible. Client UI is spacious, editorial, image-led. Do not fork the system.
03
Hotspots are the signature
Tap a zone on a render, the scope and price rise. Spend the animation budget here. Keep everything else quiet.
04
Money is always tabular
Every dollar figure sets in IBM Plex Mono at tabular-nums. Non-negotiable — this product is about trust.
05
Copper is the CTA voice
One accent color, used for exactly one thing per surface: what needs to happen next. Not decoration.
06
We are not a design tool
We never generate renders. Contractors upload from VizTerra, SketchUp, Lumion. Our job starts where theirs stops.

Naming · decision still open
Working brand.
ProposalCraft describes phase one of three; by the time the build loop ships, the name will feel small. The product is now a project workspace, not an estimating tool. Foundry is our working brand — it evokes craft and forming and doesn't box in what the product becomes. Alternatives worth considering below. Decide before you buy a domain and build brand assets.

Copy voice
How we sound.
✓ Say this
"Concrete pour starts Wednesday."
"Pick your patio pavers."
"Two days ahead of schedule."
"Marcus suggests a cedar pergola."
"Send estimate."
✗ Not this
"Milestone deliverable Q2 pending."
"Please make your paver selection."
"Project on schedule per plan."
"Change order suggestion available."
"Submit quote for review."
Active voice. A button says exactly what happens — "Send estimate," not "Submit." The action keeps its name through the whole flow. Errors say what happened and how to fix it. Empty states are invitations, not decoration.
